Install Typewriter on iPhone / iPad

Follow these quick steps in Apple Safari to install Typewriter on your device home screen:

1. Tap the Share button (box with upward arrow ⎋) in your Safari toolbar.

2. Scroll down the menu and tap Add to Home Screen.

3. Tap Add in the top-right corner to finish installing.
